The idea

Same product, wildly different prices, because sellers count on you buying alone.

Two neighbours can pay completely different amounts for the exact same service. Whollar fixes the thing that causes it.

Alone

You pay the loyalty tax

Prices drift up every renewal and vary house to house. Sellers bank on you not comparing, and not leaving.

Together

You become leverage

A hundred buyers in one area is volume. Volume changes the offer a seller will make. That is just how buying works.

Whollar

We run the cohort

We gather the group, take its demand to sellers, and bring back offers you confirm on your own terms. No haggling, no 40 minute calls.
