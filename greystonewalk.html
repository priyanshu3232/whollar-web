How the cohort works

Join now, decide later, pay nothing either way

Joining is free and non binding. You only make a decision when you are holding an offer with your own name and price on it.

  1. Now01Forming

    Residents join, submit a bill, rate their provider.

  2. Next02Threshold

    At 30% of doors the building is big enough to take to providers as one purchase.

  3. Then03Sealed bids

    Providers bid without seeing each other. They see aggregates only, never who you are.

  4. Then04Your offer

    Each household sees its own price and takes it or leaves it.

  5. Finally05Switch

    Whollar handles the cancellation, the install window and the promo timing.
