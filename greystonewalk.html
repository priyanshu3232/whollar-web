What a provider sees

They bid on a building, not on you

A provider bidding for Greystone Walk is given enough to price the work and nothing that identifies anyone in it. This is the whole trade, so it is worth being exact about.

What they are given

  • How many households are in the cohort, and which tower each is in
  • The spread of speeds households are on today, as a distribution
  • The region, Scarborough Southwest, and the postal areas it covers
  • When the cohort's renewal dates cluster, so the switch can be timed

What they are never given

  • Your name, your unit number or your email address
  • Your bill, or anyone's individual bill
  • Another provider's bid, its count, or that another provider bid at all
  • Your address, until you have accepted an offer and released it yourself

A bid is sealed: it is placed once, revised only by replacing it, and never withdrawn. Read the privacy policy for the full account.

What we do, so you are not doing it alone

You are not selling anything and you are not handling anyone's bill. Whollar writes the notice, checks which providers can actually reach the towers, runs the bidding, and answers anything technical a neighbour asks. Your part is the part only a resident can do, which is being a resident people recognise.

If more than one person in a tower wants the seat, we will say so plainly and let the tower decide rather than choosing for it.

Endorsements, ratings and referral standings are member features and are not shown on this page.
