# Redirect map, September 2026: whollar.ca to internet.whollar.ca

Generated by the Phase D step of the domain restructure from the sitemap saved
verbatim on 2026-09-02 (26 URLs) plus the unlisted paths named in the brief and
the eleven legacy rules the old config carried. Implemented in `home/vercel.json`,
verified by `scripts/check-redirect-map.mjs`.

**This map is permanent.** Every rule below is a 301 or a 308 and none is to be
removed. Rankings, sent emails and bookmarks depend on it. A 404 on the
umbrella project is a missing rule: add it here and in the config, never revert.

Canonical host is `www.whollar.ca`; the apex 308s to www (host rule, first in
the config). So every indexed legacy URL reaches the product host in ONE hop and
an apex form in two. Nothing redirects to a redirect on the destination host.

## Not redirected, deliberately

- `/disclosures` keeps its 307. It was never permanent: it points at `/terms`
  only until a disclosures page exists, and a 301 there would be cached by
  browsers past the day that page ships.
- `/hooks/zeptomail` is a REWRITE to the Catalyst function, because ZeptoMail POSTs
  to it and will not follow a redirect. Owner step in the runbook: repoint the
  webhook at the internet host, then this rewrite can go.
- Asset paths (`/images/*`, `/js/*`, `/fonts/*`, `/css/*`) are not mapped. The
  umbrella serves its own; a stale asset URL from a cached old page 404s, which
  is correct and harmless.
