While we add it up, here is the whole idea.

1

On your own, you get the individual price list. A discount to sign you, then the real rate once it runs out. No call, no letter, just a bigger number for the rest of the term.

2

Nothing announces the day it changes. The number moves, autopay clears it, and most households find out two or three bills later.

3

On Whollar, your street pools into one cohort. Providers bid for the whole block, and a block does not get quietly reset. One rate, no cheap half and expensive half.

4

You take the best offer, or stay put. Switching fees are $0 by law, so the only way to lose is to not be in the room.
