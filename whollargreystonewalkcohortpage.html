Questions residents ask

The things people check before they join

Does the building have a bulk internet agreement?

We confirm this with each condo corporation separately. Three buildings, three answers. If a bulk agreement is in force, the cohort runs as an add on for households who want a different provider.

Will someone need to drill into my unit?

It depends on the independent's technology in your tower. Where fibre already reaches the riser, the install is usually one visit to the existing telecom outlet. We post the details per building once the site survey is done.

What happens to my TV or home phone bundle?

Your offer shows the internet only price. Before you decide, we tell you what your current provider will charge for the rest of the bundle once internet leaves it.

Who sees my bill?

Only Whollar. Providers see building level aggregates, counts, medians and satisfaction, never names, units or individual bills.

When is the lobby session?

Thursday Sept 11, 7 pm, in the Building 3 party room. RSVP through your campaign manager.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. Providers pay Whollar only when they win a building. Since June 2026, activation, change and cancellation fees are banned, so switching costs you nothing either.
