    The model

    Pay per delivered household

    Confirmed counts set your volume tiers and sharpen your bids. The invoice line is the completed switch: a live connection at the cohort rate. Fees accrue at completion and invoice monthly, with a 14 day reconciliation window.

    Reconciliation

    Dispute a line, keep it simple

    Every invoice line names a completion date and an anonymized household reference. Flag any line within 14 days and it holds out of the total until resolved.
