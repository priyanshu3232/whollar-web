Reading your tire size

Look for a code like 225/45R17 in two places:

  • On the sidewall of your current tire.
  • On the sticker inside the driver's door jamb, which lists the factory size.

225 is the width in mm, 45 is the sidewall as a percent of width, 17 is the wheel in inches. Still stuck? Pick "Not sure" and we will handle it.
